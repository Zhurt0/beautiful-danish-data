Copenhagen and Aarhus do two kinds of economics

Of the 174 papers on this site, 72 have an author based at the University of Copenhagen and none at Aarhus University, and 31 have an author at Aarhus and none at Copenhagen. Only 6 have authors from both. The two groups work with the same registers but ask different questions: Copenhagen papers are more often about taxation, Aarhus papers about education.

Same journals, different topics

The two groups reach the Top 5 journals at similar rates: 18 of 72 Copenhagen papers (25%) and 6 of 31 Aarhus papers (19%), a difference that is not statistically significant (Fisher exact p = 0.62).

How papers are assigned: an author's affiliation is the first institution listed for them on the Author network page. A paper counts for a university if at least one of its authors is based there, so co-authors from other institutions do not change the assignment. The comparison covers only papers published in the journals on this list, and affiliations taken from publication records can be out of date.
