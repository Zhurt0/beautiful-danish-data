Research assistants and where they went

The acknowledgments of 36 papers thank someone for research assistance, out of the 169 whose full text could be read. They name 77 people. A web search found what 48 of them did next: most went on to a PhD, and many are now on a university faculty.

Latest position found for each of the 77 people, from university pages, personal sites and publication records. The search relied on search results and could not open most pages, so some positions may be out of date. People who were not found are mostly student assistants in Denmark with no public profile; that says nothing about how they fared, and it tilts the picture towards academia. Names and individual positions are not published here.

Which papers thank research assistants

Thanks for research assistance are concentrated in the Top 5 and in papers written across borders: 31 of the 107 papers with authors in Denmark and abroad thank an assistant (29%), against 3 of 35 with authors in Denmark only and 2 of 27 with authors abroad only.

From research assistant to author

  • Rasmus Lentz was thanked in an early version of Christensen et al. (2005), became one of its authors, and is also an author of Lentz and Mortensen (2008), Lentz et al. (2023).
