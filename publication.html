What goes with a Top 5 publication

Of the 184 papers on this site, 49 appeared in a Top 5 journal. The table asks which features of a paper go with being one of them. Papers with an author at a leading department, with larger samples and with more citations are more often in the Top 5. The seniority of the authors makes no detectable difference.

How to read it

These are associations within a selected group, not causes. Every paper here was already published in a good journal, so the comparison is Top 5 against other good journals. Citations are counted after publication and a Top 5 publication itself brings citations, so column (5) describes the papers and does not predict anything in advance. Replication packages are required by the Top 5 journals, so that row may also reflect the outcome.

The models predict poorly. Column (5) classifies 75.5% of papers correctly out of sample, against 73.4% for always guessing that a paper is not in the Top 5.
