What goes with a Top 5 publication

Of the 184 papers on this site, 49 appeared in a Top 5 journal. The charts ask which features of a paper go with being one of them. Papers with an author at a leading department, with larger samples and with more citations are more often in the Top 5. The seniority of the authors makes no detectable difference.

Most papers are written with someone abroad

Of the 184 papers on this site, 141 (77%) have at least one author based outside Denmark, and 43 (23%) are written entirely by authors based in Denmark. Teams based only in Denmark are rare in the Top 5 (5 of 49 papers) and common in the Journal of Public Economics (12 of 25).

How to read it

These are associations within a selected group, not causes. Every paper here was already published in a good journal, so the comparison is Top 5 against other good journals. Citations are counted after publication and a Top 5 publication itself brings citations, so column (5) describes the papers and does not predict anything in advance. Replication packages are required by the Top 5 journals, so that row may also reflect the outcome.

The models predict poorly. Column (5) classifies 75.0% of papers correctly out of sample, against 73.4% for always guessing that a paper is not in the Top 5.

Means by group; for yes-or-no variables the mean is the share of papers. The median paper outside the Top 5 has 44 citations and the median Top 5 paper 197. Author locations come from the affiliations on each paper in OpenAlex where available, and otherwise from the Author network page; the leading-department, Copenhagen and Aarhus variables use every institution listed for an author on that page.
