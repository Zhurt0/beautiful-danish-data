Survey

Many papers link register data to a survey through the personal identifier: the Labour Force Survey for hours and occupations, the Expenditure Survey to validate imputed consumption, children's cohort surveys, election studies, or a survey the authors fielded themselves. This page collects the questionnaires behind those surveys, so you can see exactly what respondents were asked.
