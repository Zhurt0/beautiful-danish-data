A small share of Denmark's economists write these papers

About 873 researchers in Denmark publish mainly in economics today. 72 of them, or 8%, are an author of at least one paper on this site. Set against the number of economists, the University of Copenhagen has the most papers: 44 for every hundred, against 10 at Copenhagen Business School.

The two sides of the ratio are measured differently, so read it as a rough guide. Economics researchers are people in OpenAlex whose latest institution is in Denmark, who have at least five works of which 40% or more are in economics, econometrics and finance, and who have published since 2022. That counts PhD students and visitors with enough papers and misses people OpenAlex files under another field. Papers are the papers on this site with an author who lists the institution on the Author network page, at any time since 1998, so they include work by people who have since left. A paper with authors at two institutions counts for both, and the Denmark row counts each paper once.

By field of the researcher

Here the people are sorted by field, not the papers. Each of the 867 economics researchers is placed in the field where most of their own publications fall, and the chart shows what share of each field has written a paper on this site. Labour economists are the most likely to have one (21 of 60). Finance is the largest field in Denmark, with 239 researchers, and almost none of them work with the registers in these journals.

A researcher's field is read from the topics OpenAlex assigns to their publications, grouped into the fields above by keyword, and taken as the group with the most publications. The topics are coarse and sometimes wrong: work on risk preferences, for example, is often filed under health, and education research mostly sits outside economics in OpenAlex, which is why that field is so small here. 13 researchers whose topics fit none of the fields are left out, and topics were missing for 6 of the 873 researchers counted above. Treat the shares as a rough guide.
