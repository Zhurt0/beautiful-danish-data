Variable map

Statistics Denmark delivers data as registers, one per topic, that all share the personal identifier (PNR) on the individual side and the firm identifier (CVRNR) on the firm side. The two sides are joined through the employer link in IDA, FIDA or the monthly payroll data. The map below lists the registers behind the papers on this site, the variables that matter most, and which papers use each register.

Variable names follow Statistics Denmark's documentation of high-quality variables, but names and version suffixes (such as _13) change between years and projects. Treat the codes as pointers and check the current documentation at dst.dk before writing code.

Linking the two

You haveYou wantGo through
A worker (PNR)Their employer's accountsIDAN or FIDA gives CVRNR for the November job; BFL gives it monthly. Then join FIRM/FIRE on CVRNR and year.
A firm (CVRNR)All its workers and their wagesFIDA or IDAN filtered on CVRNR gives the PNR set. Join IND for annual earnings or BFL for monthly wages and hours.
A firmIts CEO's or owner's familyCompany registers give the CEO's PNR; BEF gives parents, spouse and children.
A workerCo-workers at the same establishmentIDAN on ARBNR and year, not CVRNR, since a firm can have many workplaces.
A worker's job historyFirm-level trade exposureJob spells from IDAN/BFL joined to UHDI on CVRNR and year, as in Hummels et al. (2014).
